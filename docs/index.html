How to Use This App

Search: Find any player to see their stats, team, position, and role (starter/rotation/bench). All data comes from nflverse official releases.

Weekly Predictions: View model predictions based on recent performance. Predictions use empirical Bayes methods on trailing usage stats. ESPN and Vegas require manual CSV upload (not included).

Build My Card: Create a PrizePicks-style card with 2-6 players from any teams. Your selections are saved locally in your browser.

Model Quality: Week 2 grades show overall MAE of 10.2 with 77% of predictions within 1 standard deviation. Passing yards and receptions are the toughest to predict.

About This Data

Zero fabricated numbers. Every stat comes from nflverse public data releases. If data is missing, it's marked as "not loaded" rather than invented.

Sources: Player stats, snap counts, injuries, depth charts, and team data from nflverse via the nflreadpy package.

Player photos: ESPN CDN URLs derived from nflverse player IDs. Team logos from nflverse team metadata.
